We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you:

We may use your information to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests. We may also use it to protect our rights, privacy, safety, and property, as well as those of our users and the public.

Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience and collect information about how you use our services. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and track your activity.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies:

    Required for basic website functionality, such as authentication and security.

  • Performance Cookies:

    Help us understand how users interact with our website and improve performance.

  • Functional Cookies:

    Remember your preferences and personalize your experience on our website.

  • Marketing Cookies:

    Track your activity to deliver targeted advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can choose to accept, reject, or delete cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your ability to access certain features.

For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use our services at your own risk.
